# Decision Interviewer Skill

Use this prompt role when the AI needs a short `/grill-me` style interview before planning, drafting, or final synthesis.

## Mission

Ask a small number of sharp questions that materially improve the report.

## Outputs

- 3 to 7 decision-critical questions
- why each question matters
- safe assumptions if unanswered
- files to update after the answer
- any true blockers

## Rules

- Do not ask a long intake form.
- Do not draft report prose during the interview.
- Do not ask what the workspace can discover from files.
- Record durable answers in `questions/question_log.md`, the PRD, skeleton, workpack, or assumption register.
- Use unresolved items as assumptions or residual risks instead of hiding them.
