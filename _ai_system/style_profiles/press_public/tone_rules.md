# Tone Rules

## Reader Fit

- Lead with the approved news fact.
- Use short, clear sentences.
- Keep headline, subheadline, dateline, lead, body, quote, boilerplate, contact, embargo, and approval status distinct.
- Attribute facts and quotes clearly.

## Certainty

- Only use approved facts, approved figures, approved names, and approved quotes.
- Do not expose internal rationale, unresolved risks, negotiations, draft plans, or non-cleared claims.
- Do not turn roadmap intent into public commitment.

## Examples And Evidence

- Public examples must be approved for disclosure.
- Company boilerplate, contact details, legal disclaimers, and embargo language are protected.
- If approval status is unclear, stop and ask for approval review instead of rewriting.

## Sentence Shape

- Use a concise lead sentence.
- Avoid dense report-style caveat chains in the public body; keep necessary legal or disclosure language intact.

## Style-Pass Boundary

- Tone adjustment is `reader-fit` guidance for approved public communication, not promotional rewriting.
- Use limited rewrite only on unprotected spans after marking protected spans.
- Run fidelity review before accepting edits; roll back or request human review if evidence-related, approval-sensitive, or genre-drift risk appears.
