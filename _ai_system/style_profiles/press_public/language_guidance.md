# Press Public Language Guidance

Use this file as a reader-fit layer for `press_public`. It does not translate, rewrite automatically, verify claims, or replace approvals.

## English Reader-Fit Guidance

- Write for English-reading public or media audiences who expect approved facts, approved quotes, clear datelines, plain boilerplate, and precise contact/embargo details.
- English public copy should be plain, quotable, and concise. Approved facts and approved quotes should drive the text.
- Use press-release conventions such as dateline, direct quote blocks, boilerplate, and media contact only when those elements are confirmed.
- This is not a translation of a Korean profile. It is a reader-fit overlay for English public documents.

## English Writing Boundary

- Do not invent approvals, quotes, embargo text, contact details, company boilerplate, or regulatory status.
- Do not convert internal caveats into public claims.
- Mechanical-tone reduction means removing vagueness and preserving approved meaning, not adding promotional language.
- Avoid generic public copy that sounds polished but hides source, approval, quote, contact, or review status.

## Protected Spans

Preserve approved quotes, names, titles, dates, product names, legal/regulatory language, public boilerplate, contact details, embargo text, source-backed claims, and approval wording.
