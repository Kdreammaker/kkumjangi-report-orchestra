# Forbidden Patterns

## Stock Or Hollow Formality

- `학술적으로 매우 중요한 함의를 가진다` without specifying contribution.
- `다양한 관점에서 종합적으로 분석하였다` without method detail.
- Overuse of nominalized phrases that hide actor, method, or evidence.

## Genre Drift

- Turning a paper-style document into a consulting recommendation memo.
- Turning limitations into business caveats.
- Mixing source paraphrase with the author's interpretation.

## Overclaim

- Causal claims from descriptive data.
- General claims from narrow examples.
- Literature-review statements without citations.
- `입증했다`, `검증되었다`, `보편적이다` unless the method supports that level.

## Protected Content

- Do not rewrite direct quotes, translated source passages, citation details, method names, statistical values, article numbers, law names, proper nouns, or source-backed claims.
- Do not change hedging if the hedge reflects an evidence limitation.

## Style-Pass Risk Labels

- Use `style-only` for local wording, rhythm, connector, or hollow-formality issues.
- Use `evidence-related` when method, claim type, causal language, evidence strength, limitation, source relation, or citation meaning may change.
- Use `approval-sensitive` for legal, public, disclosure, disclaimer, confidential, or owner-approved wording inside a research document.
- Use `genre-drift` when a paper-like document becomes a consulting memo, sales pitch, or opinion essay.
- Use `reader-fit` when the expert reader cannot see definition, method, finding, interpretation, limitation, or citation role.
