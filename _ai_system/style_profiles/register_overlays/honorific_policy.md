# Korean Honorific And 압존법 Policy

This policy applies when a Korean register overlay may affect honorifics, job titles, institutional references, or hierarchical speech. It is guidance-only and does not automatically rewrite text.

## Core Position

압존법 is conditional, not the default. Do not apply it across all Korean documents, and do not treat it as a universal correctness rule.

Use honorific and title handling to protect clarity, respect, institutional accuracy, approval wording, and reader trust. When hierarchy, organization norms, or speaking context is unclear, hold the change and request human review.

## Apply, Do Not Apply, Or Hold

| Situation | Default action |
|---|---|
| Written report, analysis, proposal, public statement, policy review | Do not over-apply 압존법. Use neutral titles and institution/person names consistently. |
| Spoken briefing inside the same organization, with senior listener and internal hierarchy known | Consider 압존법 only if Korean workplace speech norms and the speaker/listener/person relationship require it. |
| Meeting script involving client, partner, regulator, press, or external institution | Prefer respectful title/name clarity over internal 압존법. Do not lower the status of external parties through internal hierarchy logic. |
| Approved public quote, press quote, legal phrase, contract-like phrase, official notice, or approval wording | Do not change. Preserve exactly unless the owner approves a source-checked correction. |
| Unknown hierarchy, mixed organizations, unclear audience, sensitive personnel wording | Hold for human review or ask the user. |

## Written Report Standard

In written reports, 압존법 should usually not drive sentence rewriting. Prefer:

- `홍길동 대표는 ...라고 밝혔습니다.`
- `A기관은 ...를 승인했습니다.`
- `고객사는 ...를 요청했습니다.`
- `김OO 팀장은 ...를 검토했습니다.`

Avoid forcing spoken hierarchy into written analysis:

- Do not remove necessary titles only because the reader is senior.
- Do not downgrade references to a person or institution in a public, partner, legal, or evidence-sensitive document.
- Do not change approved wording to make internal hierarchy sound natural.

## Oral Briefing Standard

In oral briefing or meeting remarks, 압존법 may require judgment when all of these are known:

- the speaker, listener, and referenced person are in a clear hierarchy;
- the organization expects Korean workplace honorific conventions;
- the text is meant to be spoken, not published as a formal report;
- changing the honorific does not alter approved wording, evidence, attribution, or responsibility.

If any condition is missing, mark the span for review instead of applying 압존법 automatically.

## Reference Principles By Relationship

| Referent | Preferred handling |
|---|---|
| Our company / internal organization | Use official company or team names in public/external documents. Internal shorthand is allowed only in internal drafts. |
| Other company / partner / customer | Preserve official names and titles. Use respectful neutral wording; do not apply internal hierarchy rules to external parties. |
| Customer / client | Use `고객`, `고객사`, official organization name, or approved customer label. Avoid casual or possessive wording. |
| Public agency / regulator / institution | Preserve official institution names, departments, program names, and titles. Do not paraphrase official phrases casually. |
| Senior leader / executive | Use official title and name when needed for clarity. In spoken internal notes, consider honorific norms only when hierarchy is clear. |
| Junior employee / contributor | Use name/title respectfully and consistently. Do not erase responsibility or authorship through vague passive wording. |

## Protected Items

Never change these for honorific or 압존법 reasons without explicit owner approval:

- job titles, role titles, department names, institution names, legal entity names, and product/service names;
- personal names and approved name/title combinations;
- approval phrases, signature blocks, official statements, press quotes, disclaimers, contract-like terms, safety warnings, and official notices;
- direct quotes, source-backed claims, law/regulation names, dates, numbers, prices, and metrics.

## Human Review Triggers

Send to human review or ask the user when:

- hierarchy is unclear or crosses organizations;
- an executive, customer, regulator, press outlet, school, hospital, court, or public agency is referenced;
- the sentence may affect responsibility, blame, approval, safety, contract, or public disclosure;
- the user has supplied exact wording, a quote, or approval text;
- a register change would make the wording sound more natural but less precise;
- 압존법 would require lowering or removing an honorific for someone named in the text.

## Examples

Written report:

- Prefer: `박민수 본부장은 신규 운영 기준을 승인했습니다.`
- Hold/review instead of forcing: `박민수 본부장이 신규 운영 기준을 승인했다` when the title/name may be approval-sensitive.

Internal oral briefing:

- Possible only with clear context: `대표님께 보고드릴 때는 김 본부장 관련 표현을 조직 관행에 맞춰 조정해야 합니다.`
- Review needed: `김 본부장이 말씀하셨습니다` versus `김 본부장이 말했습니다` when the listener, speaker, and organization norm are unknown.

External/public wording:

- Preserve: `A기관은 2026년 6월 3일 해당 지침을 공지했습니다.`
- Do not rewrite into casual speech or internal hierarchy wording.
