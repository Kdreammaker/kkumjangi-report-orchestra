# Forbidden Patterns

## Stock Or Empty Executive Prose

- `시사하는 바가 크다` without a concrete decision implication.
- `종합적으로 고려할 때` followed by a vague restatement.
- `중요한 의미를 가진다` without owner, action, or risk.
- Repetitive `첫째/둘째/셋째` when a decision table is clearer.

## Genre Drift

- Turning an internal risk memo into sales copy.
- Hiding unresolved legal or financial uncertainty in polished language.
- Expanding a short decision summary into a long essay.
- Replacing clear owners with vague passive voice.

## Overclaim

- `확실하다`, `무조건`, `완전히 해결`, `리스크 없음`.
- Strategy claims that omit assumptions, data limits, or execution conditions.
- Source-backed statements rewritten beyond the source's actual meaning.

## Protected Content

- Do not rewrite direct quotes, law names, article numbers, figures, dates, approval wording, or contract/public text.
- Do not paraphrase source-backed claim sentences if the change could alter confidence, scope, actor, timing, or obligation.

## Style-Pass Risk Labels

- Use `style-only` for local wording, rhythm, connector, or empty phrase issues.
- Use `evidence-related` when the change could alter claim type, evidence strength, caveat, uncertainty, source relation, or citation meaning.
- Use `approval-sensitive` for legal, financial, public, contract, confidentiality, disclosure, disclaimer, or owner-approved wording.
- Use `genre-drift` when the summary becomes sales copy, an essay, or a final approval memo instead of a decision summary.
- Use `reader-fit` when decision owner, action, risk, timing, or blocker visibility is poor.
