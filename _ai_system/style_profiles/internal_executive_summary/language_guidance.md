# Internal Executive Summary Language Guidance

Use this file as a reader-fit layer for `internal_executive_summary`. It does not translate, rewrite automatically, verify claims, or replace approvals.

## English Reader-Fit Guidance

- Write for English-reading executives who scan for decision, implication, confidence level, owner, and next step.
- Put the conclusion, implication, and requested decision near the top. Prefer shorter sentences and explicit verbs.
- Make caveats visible in the same sentence or immediately after the claim, especially when source freshness, legal status, distribution market, or approval status is uncertain.
- This is not a translation of a Korean profile. It is a reader-fit overlay for English executive documents.

## English Writing Boundary

- Use clear labels such as `Decision Needed`, `Key Finding`, `Implication`, `Risk`, and `Next Step` only when they fit the PRD.
- Do not soften material risks merely to sound executive-friendly.
- Reducing mechanical tone means preserving meaning, evidence status, and reader usefulness, not automatic broad rewrite.
- Avoid generic confidence language; make confidence level, evidence status, and required review concrete.

## Protected Spans

Preserve direct quotes, numbers, dates, units, financial metrics, legal/regulatory names, proper nouns, source-backed claims, and citation locators.
