# Tone Rules

## Reader Fit

- Start with the decision, current status, or recommendation.
- Keep sentences short enough for fast scanning.
- Use direct subjects: company, team, regulator, partner, customer, investor, or owner.
- State responsibility, deadline, blocker, and residual risk clearly.
- Use tables or bullets for options, risk, owner, and next action when it improves decision speed.

## Certainty

- Be direct about confirmed risks.
- Use cautious wording for incomplete evidence: `현재 확인 가능한 자료 기준`, `추가 확인 필요`, `법률 검토 필요`.
- Do not make a risk sound smaller merely to keep the summary short.

## Examples And Evidence

- Use examples only when they help the decision owner understand a tradeoff.
- Keep source-backed claims traceable to the claim register or source record.
- Do not remove caveats from legal, regulatory, financial, or approval-sensitive text.

## Sentence Shape

- Prefer one judgment per sentence.
- Split sentences that combine fact, interpretation, and recommendation.
- Use `결론`, `근거`, `리스크`, `다음 조치` blocks when useful.

## Style-Pass Boundary

- Tone adjustment is `reader-fit` guidance for decision speed, not a style-only excuse to change meaning.
- Use limited rewrite only on unprotected spans after marking protected spans.
- Run fidelity review before accepting edits; roll back or request human review if evidence-related, approval-sensitive, or genre-drift risk appears.
