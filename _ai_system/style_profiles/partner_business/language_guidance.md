# Partner Business Language Guidance

Use this file as a reader-fit layer for `partner_business`. It does not translate, rewrite automatically, verify claims, or replace approvals.

## English Reader-Fit Guidance

- Write for English-reading partners who expect the value, scope, responsibilities, exclusions, constraints, review status, and next step to be explicit.
- English prose can be warm, but should not become sales copy when evidence or approval is missing.
- Contract-like boundaries should be plain rather than implied through tone.
- This is not a translation of a Korean profile. It is a reader-fit overlay for English partner-facing documents.

## English Writing Boundary

- Prefer specific verbs: `propose`, `recommend`, `plan`, `request`, `review`, `confirm`.
- Avoid implied commitments through `will deliver`, `guarantee`, `ensure`, or `certify` unless the approved scope says so.
- Mechanical-tone reduction is a fit-for-reader check, not a general rewrite. Keep approved wording and source-backed claims intact.
- Avoid generic partnership language; state scope, evidence, owner, and approval status plainly.

## Protected Spans

Preserve direct quotes, prices, dates, schedules, deliverables, responsibilities, exclusions, liability/confidentiality wording, company names, product names, and source-backed claims.
