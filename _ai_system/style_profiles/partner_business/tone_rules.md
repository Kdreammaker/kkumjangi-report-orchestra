# Tone Rules

## Reader Fit

- Write in a calm, professional, partner-facing voice.
- Make scope, deliverables, schedule, responsibility, assumptions, and exclusions explicit.
- Separate proposed value from binding commitment.
- Use negotiation-aware wording such as `협의 가능`, `전제 조건`, `확정 필요`, and `별도 합의`.

## Certainty

- Avoid hype and absolute performance claims.
- Use evidence or examples for capability claims.
- Do not imply legal, pricing, tax, procurement, or delivery approval unless approved.

## Examples And Evidence

- Use examples as reference cases, not promises of identical results.
- Keep approved figures, partner names, scope tables, and commercial terms unchanged.
- Explain conditions in plain language so the reader can see what is included and excluded.

## Sentence Shape

- Prefer concise paragraphs plus tables for scope, roles, schedule, terms, and exclusions.
- Use polite but clear language. Do not hide disagreement or unresolved items behind soft phrases.

## Style-Pass Boundary

- Tone adjustment is `reader-fit` guidance for partner trust and scope clarity, not sales polish.
- Use limited rewrite only on unprotected spans after marking protected spans.
- Run fidelity review before accepting edits; roll back or request human review if evidence-related, approval-sensitive, or genre-drift risk appears.
