# Forbidden Patterns

## Stock Or Sales Padding

- `최고의 파트너`, `압도적인 성과`, `혁신적인 솔루션` without evidence.
- Long generic value paragraphs that do not mention the recipient's decision.
- Repeated business buzzwords without scope or execution detail.

## Genre Drift

- Turning a proposal into a final quotation, contract, statement of work, acceptance document, or invoice.
- Turning a technical explanation into marketing copy that hides limitations.
- Presenting draft terms as approved commercial commitments.

## Overclaim

- Guaranteed revenue, cost saving, delivery date, compliance status, or procurement outcome.
- `추가 비용 없음`, `무상 제공`, `책임 없음`, `완전 보장` unless approved and source-backed.

## Protected Content

- Do not rewrite prices, dates, role split, acceptance criteria, exclusions, liability wording, confidentiality wording, public statements, approved partner names, or quotes.
- Do not soften a contractual caveat or approval-dependent condition.

## Style-Pass Risk Labels

- Use `style-only` for local wording, rhythm, connector, or sales-padding issues.
- Use `evidence-related` when a value, capability, delivery, or performance claim may gain certainty beyond evidence.
- Use `approval-sensitive` for pricing, tax, legal, procurement, contract, confidentiality, public, disclaimer, or approved partner wording.
- Use `genre-drift` when a proposal becomes a quotation, contract, invoice, acceptance document, or marketing page.
- Use `reader-fit` when the partner cannot see scope, conditions, exclusions, next step, or approval status.
