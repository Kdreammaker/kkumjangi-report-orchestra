# Report Neutral Design System

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Quiet, executive, information-dense, and print-aware. The design should support serious reading and decision review rather than marketing.

## Color Palette & Roles

- Primary ink: `#172033`
- Secondary text: `#4B5565`
- Rule line: `#D7DEE8`
- Accent: `#1F6FEB`
- Soft surface: `#F6F8FB`
- Warning: `#9A5B00`
- Risk: `#B42318`

## Typography Rules

Use clear Korean body text, conservative heading scale, and tabular numerals for data. Avoid oversized hero typography inside analytical sections.

## Component Stylings

Use cover, chapter header, decision box, risk note, table, figure, and appendix components consistently.

## Layout Principles

Prefer readable page width, strong section hierarchy, and stable table/figure captions. Do not nest cards inside cards.

## Depth & Elevation

Use borders, spacing, and subtle fills. Avoid decorative blur, gradient blobs, and ornamental backgrounds.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do use visual hierarchy to make claims, evidence, and residual risks easy to scan. Do not hide uncertainty or use design to imply stronger confidence than the evidence supports.

## Responsive Behavior

The report should remain readable on desktop and printable to PDF/DOCX workflows.

## Agent Prompt Guide

Design is subordinate to report substance. Use the design system to make the argument more legible, not to compensate for weak content.
