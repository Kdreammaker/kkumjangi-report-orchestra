# Regulatory Review PRD Questions

- Which jurisdiction, regulator, law, policy, or approval path is in scope?
- Is the output legal advice, business risk review, or internal preparation material?
- Which conclusions require counsel, regulator, or expert review?
- Which official sources are mandatory before drafting?
- What product launch, licensing, investor, tax, AML, consumer, or overseas risks are affected?
- What uncertainty should remain visible to the reader?
