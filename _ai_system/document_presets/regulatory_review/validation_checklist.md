# Regulatory Review Validation Checklist

- Primary law, regulator, exchange, court, or official guidance sources are prioritized.
- Legal facts are not blended with AI/analyst interpretation.
- Counsel-review markers appear where conclusions affect launch, licensing, tax, AML, investor access, or consumer protection.
- Approval probability and compliance readiness are not overstated.
- Direct quotes include exact wording and location.
- Open issues and unresolved interpretations remain visible.
