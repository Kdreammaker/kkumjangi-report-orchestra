# Technical Design PRD Questions

- What system, feature, integration, or technology adoption is being designed or reviewed?
- Who will use the document: product, engineering, security, operations, vendor, or leadership?
- Which architecture, API, data, security, testing, deployment, or operations boundaries are in scope?
- What existing systems, dependencies, and constraints must be respected?
- What decisions must this document enable?
- Which unknowns require prototype, expert review, or implementation validation?
