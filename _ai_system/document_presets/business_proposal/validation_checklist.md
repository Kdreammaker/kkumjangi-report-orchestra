# Business Proposal Validation Checklist

- The recipient, requested decision, and next step are explicit.
- Scope, deliverables, schedule, responsibilities, and commercial terms are not ambiguous.
- Binding commitments, assumptions, and optional items are separated.
- Proposal claims about capability or value are backed by evidence or examples.
- Legal, pricing, tax, procurement, and approval caveats are visible where needed.
- The tone is appropriate for partner or customer delivery, not internal brainstorming.
- Proposal scope table, role split table, schedule, and terms/exclusions box are included when relevant.
- Cover and body hierarchy feel suitable for partner/customer delivery, not an internal memo.
- Milestone timeline, scope matrix, R&R table, and assumptions/conditions/exceptions block are present when they clarify delivery risk.
- Schedule, cost, and role tables are checked for Word page-width risk before they become dense proposal exhibits.

## Prohibited Style and Judgment Patterns

- Do not write the proposal as a final quotation, contract, statement of work, or acceptance document unless legal/commercial approval status explicitly supports that use.
- Do not present draft scope, pricing, tax handling, delivery dates, responsibilities, or exclusions as binding commitments when they are still assumptions or negotiation points.
- Do not use sales certainty that erases caveats; separate proposed value, required conditions, open issues, and approval-dependent terms.

## Design And Export-Safe Checkpoints

- AI judgment labels each schedule, cost, responsibility, and scope statement as commitment, assumption, negotiation item, or exclusion.
- AI judgment checks whether wording could be mistaken for a quotation, contract, SOW, acceptance, or invoice.
- Appendix or landscape candidates are marked as export review needs, not as guaranteed DOCX/PDF behavior.
- Deferred export-native features remain deferred: Word field codes, generated captions, page fields, landscape sections, contract automation, quotation automation, and signature workflows are not claimed.

## Export Readiness Checks

- Tables stay within Word page width and long cells, conditions, exceptions, and URLs can wrap.
- Static visuals include captions and alternative descriptions; interactive HTML, viewport-dependent layouts, and complex absolute positioning are avoided.
- DOCX/PDF export success is not assumed until export validation is run separately.
