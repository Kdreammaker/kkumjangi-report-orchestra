# Business Proposal PRD Questions

- Who is the recipient and what decision should they make after reading?
- Is this for a partner, customer, sponsor, vendor, public institution, or internal approval chain?
- What problem, opportunity, proposed solution, deliverables, schedule, roles, and commercial terms are in scope?
- Which parts are binding, indicative, optional, or subject to negotiation?
- What evidence proves capability, fit, feasibility, and expected value?
- Which confidentiality, legal review, quote validity, and approval boundaries apply?
