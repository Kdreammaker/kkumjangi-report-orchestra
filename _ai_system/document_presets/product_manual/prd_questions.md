# Product Manual PRD Questions

- What product, version, edition, platform, or release does the manual cover?
- Who is the user: end user, administrator, installer, operator, support team, or trainer?
- Which tasks, setup steps, configuration paths, workflows, and troubleshooting cases are in scope?
- What prerequisites, permissions, supported environments, and safety or caution notes are required?
- Which screenshots, diagrams, tables, FAQs, and glossary items are needed?
- What changed from previous versions and how should limitations or unsupported cases be shown?
