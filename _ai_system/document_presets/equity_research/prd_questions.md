# Equity Research PRD Questions

- Is the focus a sector, listed company, peer group, theme, or earnings event?
- Who is the reader: internal strategy team, finance team, investor, executive, or general research reader?
- Which geographies, tickers, peer companies, periods, and data sources are in scope?
- Which performance metrics, valuation methods, assumptions, and risk factors should be analyzed?
- Should the conclusion be descriptive analysis, scenario view, or internal decision support?
- What disclaimer, limitation, non-advisory wording, and data freshness requirements apply?
