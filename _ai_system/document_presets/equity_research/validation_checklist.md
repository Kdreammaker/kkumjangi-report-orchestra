# Equity Research Validation Checklist

- The document separates data, assumptions, interpretation, and limitations.
- Valuation, earnings, and market claims include source dates and data status.
- Risks and downside scenarios are treated alongside catalysts or upside cases.
- Language does not present investment advice as a certainty.
- Peer comparisons use consistent criteria and periods.
- Disclaimers and data freshness limitations are visible before final conclusions.
- Data 기준일, disclaimer box, financial table, peer table, and sensitivity table are visible where relevant.
- Peer table, valuation multiple table, sensitivity table, earnings bridge, and catalyst/risk matrix are used only when they improve the analysis.
- Wide peer, valuation, sensitivity, or earnings tables are split vertically, summarized in the body, moved to appendix detail, or marked as a landscape candidate for export review.

## Prohibited Style and Judgment Patterns

- Do not present buy, sell, hold, target price, expected return, or portfolio action language as a certain recommendation unless the approved report scope explicitly allows it.
- Do not collapse assumptions, analyst interpretation, and source facts into one conclusion; keep valuation scenarios and sensitivity ranges visibly conditional.
- Do not write as if data freshness, model reliability, or downside risks are resolved when they remain uncertain or unverified.

## Design And Export-Safe Checkpoints

- AI judgment records peer-set choice, comparison limits, and whether valuation outputs are data, assumptions, or interpretation.
- AI judgment excludes target price, rating, buy/sell/hold, or portfolio-action language unless the approved scope explicitly allows it.
- Appendix or landscape candidates are marked as export review needs, not as guaranteed DOCX/PDF behavior.
- Deferred export-native features remain deferred: Word field codes, generated captions, page fields, landscape sections, rating templates, and market-data refresh automation are not claimed.

## Export Readiness Checks

- Wide tables are split or constrained so they remain readable within Word page width.
- Charts, screenshots, and tables use captions, source dates, and alternative descriptions.
- Interactive HTML, viewport-dependent layouts, and complex absolute positioning are avoided for exportable outputs.
- DOCX/PDF export success is not assumed until export validation is run separately.
