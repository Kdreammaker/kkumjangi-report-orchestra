# Academic Research PRD Questions

- What is the research question or synthesis question?
- Is this a literature review, research memo, thesis-style draft, or academic-paper-like artifact?
- Which methods, databases, or source inclusion criteria apply?
- What citation style, language, and bibliography expectations should be followed?
- Which counterarguments, limitations, and reproducibility notes are required?
- What evidence is outside scope or too weak for citation?
