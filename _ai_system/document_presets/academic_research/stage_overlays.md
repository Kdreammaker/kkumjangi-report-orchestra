# Academic Research Stage Overlays

## TOC

- Include research question, method or search boundary, literature map, analysis, counterarguments, and limitations.
- Avoid structuring as a business recommendation unless the PRD asks for applied implications.

## Workpack

- Track literature traceability, method notes, inclusion/exclusion logic, and limitations.
- Separate source summaries from interpretation and synthesis.

## Visual/Data

- Prefer literature maps, method flow diagrams, concept matrices, and evidence tables.
- Reproducible datasets or extraction tables should accompany quantitative summaries.
