# Academic Research Validation Checklist

- The research question and source inclusion boundary are explicit.
- Literature summaries preserve source meaning and do not invent consensus.
- Counterarguments, limitations, and method constraints are visible.
- Citations are traceable to source records and exact source locations where needed.
- Claims distinguish source fact, synthesis, inference, and unresolved issue.
- Academic-paper-like outputs do not claim journal readiness without style and field-specific review.
