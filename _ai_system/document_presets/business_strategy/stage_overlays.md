# Business Strategy Stage Overlays

## TOC

- Lead with the decision context, not background history.
- Map each 대목차 to an option, scenario, market/competitive force, execution path, or risk.

## Workpack

- Require explicit decision use, business implication, alternatives, and residual risk.
- Keep recommendation language tied to evidence strength and stated assumptions.

## Visual/Data

- Prefer option matrices, scenario tables, market maps, timelines, and risk-return views.
- Quantitative business effects require data sources plus assumptions.
