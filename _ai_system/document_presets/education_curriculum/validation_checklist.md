# Education Curriculum Validation Checklist

- Learner profile, level, delivery mode, session count, and duration are explicit.
- Each lesson has measurable learning objectives.
- Activities, assignments, and assessments align with the stated objectives.
- Instructor notes and learner handouts are separated when both are requested.
- Timing, materials, and facilitation constraints are practical.
- Assessment criteria and completion expectations are clear.
- Session table, instructor notes, learner handouts, rubric, and assessment tables are visibly separated.
- Teacher guide, student handout, activity, materials, assessment, and reflection blocks are chosen for the requested audience and delivery mode.
- Child-facing learner materials use clearer visual anchors and simpler instructions than teacher-only guidance when children are the audience.
- Rubrics, session tables, and materials lists are split by lesson, competency, activity, or audience when a single table becomes too wide.
- Tables remain readable within Word page width, and long activity prompts, rubric cells, and URLs can wrap.
- Static diagrams, images, and handout visuals include captions and alternative descriptions.
- Interactive HTML, viewport-dependent layouts, and complex absolute positioning are avoided for exportable outputs.
- DOCX/PDF export success is not assumed until export validation is run separately.

## Design And Export-Safe Checkpoints

- AI judgment records whether the output is a teacher guide, student handout, mixed curriculum pack, assignment, or assessment pack.
- AI judgment separates learner-facing blocks from teacher-only facilitation notes before layout.
- Appendix or split-table candidates are marked as export review needs, not as guaranteed DOCX/PDF behavior.
- Deferred export-native features remain deferred: Word field codes, generated captions, page fields, running headers, automatic teacher/student packet splitting, LMS packaging, and worksheet automation are not claimed.
