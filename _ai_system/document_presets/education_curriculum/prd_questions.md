# Education Curriculum PRD Questions

- Who are the learners and what prior knowledge, role, age group, or proficiency level should be assumed?
- Is the output a full curriculum, lesson plan, instructor guide, learner handout, workshop material, or assessment pack?
- How many sessions, what duration, and what delivery mode are expected?
- What learning objectives, activities, assignments, assessments, and completion criteria are required?
- What materials, tools, readings, datasets, or classroom constraints are in scope?
- Should instructor notes and learner-facing handouts be separated?
