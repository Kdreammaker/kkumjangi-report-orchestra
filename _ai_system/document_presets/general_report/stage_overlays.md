# General Report Stage Overlays

## TOC

- Keep the 대목차 tied to reader questions, not fixed chapter counts.
- Include evidence needs, counterarguments, visual intent, and residual risks for each 대목차.

## Workpack

- Require source ids, claim ids, counterpoints, and visual/data needs before drafting a substantial chapter.
- Keep confirmed facts, interpretations, estimates, and unresolved issues separate.

## Visual/Data

- Use charts, timelines, matrices, or diagrams when they clarify magnitude, sequence, dependency, or trade-off.
- Any material quantitative value needs a local CSV/XLSX or equivalent data artifact.
