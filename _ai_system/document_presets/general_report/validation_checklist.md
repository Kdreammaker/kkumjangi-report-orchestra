# General Report Validation Checklist

- The PRD states purpose, reader, scope, evidence standard, confidentiality, and output boundary.
- The TOC covers the core reader questions without hiding required 중목차/소목차.
- Source records, claim register, and source index agree before review-candidate or closeout.
- Claims distinguish direct quote, paraphrase, data-based statement, and inference.
- Visuals have reader-facing captions and backing data where needed.
- Residual risks and unverified assumptions remain visible.
