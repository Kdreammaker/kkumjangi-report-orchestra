# Investor Brief PRD Questions

- Who is the investor audience: existing shareholders, prospective investors, lenders, strategic partners, or internal IR reviewers?
- Is the output a narrative IR brief, investor factbook, diligence memo, or investor Q&A brief?
- Which company facts, business model, market, financial, traction, and governance topics must be covered?
- Which metrics are audited, management-provided, estimated, or forward-looking?
- What questions or objections should the brief prepare the reader to answer?
- What confidentiality, approval, disclaimer, and external-sharing boundaries apply?
- Should the document avoid any slide-deck, pitch-deck, or presentation-style structure?
