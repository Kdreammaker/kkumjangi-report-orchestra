# Investor Brief Stage Overlays

## Workflow Mode And Stage Depth

- Default mode: `standard`, upgraded to `substantial` when the PRD requires deep diligence, detailed financial analysis, or broad external distribution.
- Use PRD, basis-date and approved-metric confirmation, reading brief outline, source/claim mapping, section drafting, investor-risk review, style pass, versioning, and delivery readiness.
- Replace report chapter workpacks with factbook, Q&A, KPI, risk, governance, and diligence-item sections when the artifact is a concise investor brief.
- Keep Chapter 0 and a full appendix optional unless the artifact is a substantial investor review document.
- If the brief is derived from another artifact, reconfirm approval status for financials, traction, customer names, forecasts, fundraising context, and forward-looking statements before drafting.

## TOC

- Structure the document as a reading brief or factbook, not as an IR deck.
- Include company overview, business model, market context, performance facts, strategy, risks, governance or funding context, and investor Q&A when relevant.
- Separate confirmed facts, management outlook, assumptions, and open diligence items.

## Workpack

- Require source status for financial, market, customer, product, and governance claims.
- Mark forward-looking statements and scenario assumptions separately from historical facts.
- Keep promotional language subordinate to evidence, approval status, and stated limitations.
- If a Korean tone pass is requested, route it to the common style workflow and selected style profile; keep this preset focused on structure, evidence, visuals, and approval boundaries.

## Visual/Data

- Prefer fact tables, KPI trend charts, market maps, cap table or funding summaries when approved, risk matrices, and Q&A tables.
- Do not convert the document into slide headlines or presentation notes.

## Layout/Export

- Build a document hierarchy around fact table, KPI trend, risk, and investor Q&A sections; avoid pitch-deck rhythm, oversized claims, or slide-style headline blocks.
- Use `executive_decision` as the default cover for controlled investor review; switch to `public_release` only when the brief is approved for broad external sharing.
- Keep tables within Word page width, split dense KPI or cap-table data into readable sections, and make long company names, URLs, and table cells wrap.
- Prefer static charts/images with captions and alternative descriptions; avoid interactive HTML, viewport-dependent layouts, and complex absolute positioning.
- DOCX/PDF conversion requires separate export validation; this preset improves structure but does not guarantee conversion success.
