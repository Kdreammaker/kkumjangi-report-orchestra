# Investor Brief Validation Checklist

- The output is a document-style investor brief, factbook, or Q&A brief, not a slide deck.
- Financial, market, customer, and governance claims have clear source or approval status.
- Historical facts, forward-looking assumptions, and management opinions are separated.
- Investor Q&A content answers likely diligence questions without overclaiming.
- Confidentiality, external-sharing, and approval status are visible.
- Risk, limitation, and uncertainty language is not buried after promotional claims.
- Cover choice matches sharing mode: `executive_decision` for controlled investor review, `public_release` only for approved broad external release.
- Fact tables, KPI trends, risk sections, and Q&A blocks have a clear document hierarchy without pitch-deck tone.
- KPI strips or cards include metric period, unit, source, approval status, and public/confidential sharing boundary.
- Investor Q&A, risk block, and fact table placement supports diligence reading rather than persuasion-first sequencing.
- Wide KPI, funding, or cap-table material is reduced to a body summary or moved to appendix detail when needed.

## Prohibited Style and Judgment Patterns

- Do not write in pitch-deck exaggeration mode: avoid inflated traction, market-size, moat, or exit claims unless they are sourced, approved, and framed with assumptions.
- Do not turn investor diligence material into fundraising persuasion; the brief must help readers verify facts and ask questions before it asks them to believe the story.
- Do not bury unresolved risks, governance gaps, missing approvals, or weak data behind optimistic headline claims.

## Design And Export-Safe Checkpoints

- AI judgment identifies which metrics are public-approved, confidential, diligence-only, or excluded before layout.
- AI judgment separates body-level investor summary from appendix tables for cap table, KPI, funding, and financial detail.
- Appendix or landscape candidates are marked as export review needs, not as guaranteed DOCX/PDF behavior.
- Deferred export-native features remain deferred: Word field codes, generated captions, page fields, landscape sections, and investor-package automation are not claimed.

## Export Readiness Checks

- Tables, charts, screenshots, long URLs, and long text cells are designed to wrap within Word page width.
- Static assets include captions and alternative descriptions; interactive HTML, viewport-dependent layouts, and complex absolute positioning are avoided.
- DOCX/PDF export success is not assumed until export validation is run separately.
