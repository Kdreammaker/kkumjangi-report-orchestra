# Book Manuscript Validation Checklist

- The manuscript type, reader, editorial depth, output target, and verification path are explicit.
- Front matter, body matter, back matter, appendices, and revision notes are present when needed.
- Original prose, dialogue, names, canon, terminology, citations, examples, and protected spans are preserved at the required strength.
- Fiction, nonfiction, professional, textbook, and workbook conventions are not mixed without purpose.
- Editorial notes, producer notes, source notes, and reader-facing prose are visually distinct.
- The selected list preset is recorded when nested hierarchy matters.
- Tables, figures, exercises, sidebars, notes, references, and glossary entries are export-friendly.
- No final publishing-readiness claim is made without separate export and publishing checks.

## Prohibited Style and Judgment Patterns

- Do not rewrite a manuscript when the user requested format-only cleanup.
- Do not force report-style recommendations, executive summaries, or evidence registers into fiction or literary manuscripts unless requested.
- Do not remove continuity notes, citations, or source/provenance markers without approval.
- Do not present a DOCX/HTML file as publisher-ready just because the export exists.

## Design And Export-Safe Checkpoints

- AI judgment records the editorial depth: formatting-only, light cleanup, structure repair, copyedit, line edit, substantive rewrite, or new drafting.
- AI judgment records whether material is reader prose, editor note, producer note, source note, footnote/endnote, appendix, glossary, or revision log.
- `formal_outline`, `symbol_bullets`, `guide_outline`, or `procedure_steps` is selected where nested lists matter.
- Deferred export-native features remain deferred: print-ready typesetting, ebook packaging, imposition, automatic index, running heads, cross-references, generated captions, and Word style template automation are not claimed.

## Export Readiness Checks

- Inline-first HTML is used for headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, captions, callouts, sidebars, and notes when Word import is expected.
- Native DOCX export preserves chapter hierarchy and multi-level list numbering where supported.
- Static images and simple tables are used instead of decorative CSS-only manuscript effects.
- DOCX/PDF/Google Docs export success is not assumed until export validation is run separately.
