# Academic Paper PRD Questions

- What is the research question, thesis, hypothesis, or contribution?
- Is the output a seminar paper, journal-style article, thesis chapter, working paper, or academic review?
- Which discipline, citation style, language, and target reader should be assumed?
- What methodology, dataset, case material, experiment, literature base, or theoretical framework is in scope?
- What abstract, introduction, literature review, methods, findings, discussion, limitation, and reference requirements apply?
- Which claims require direct citations, methodological caveats, or limitations?
