# Academic Paper Stage Overlays

## Workflow Mode And Stage Depth

- Default mode: `substantial`, with specialized academic-genre boundaries.
- Use PRD, research question/method confirmation, outline, literature/source mapping, section drafting, citation/method review, style pass, versioning, and delivery readiness.
- Replace business-report workpacks with academic sections such as abstract, research question, contribution, method, literature review, findings, discussion, limitations, and references.
- Keep recommendation-style executive summary optional; use it only when the PRD asks for applied implications.
- If the paper is derived from another artifact, reconfirm citation status, method fit, claim originality, data limits, and reference style before reusing analysis.

## TOC

- Include abstract, research question, contribution, literature review, methods, findings or analysis, discussion, limitations, and references when applicable.
- Make the argument structure match the chosen academic genre and discipline.

## Workpack

- Require citation status, method boundaries, sample/data limits, alternative interpretations, and contribution claims.
- Keep literature summaries separate from the author's analysis and inference.

## Visual/Data

- Prefer method diagrams, conceptual frameworks, evidence tables, coding/category tables, data summaries, and limitation matrices.
- Do not use business-style recommendation visuals unless the paper genre calls for them.

## Layout/Export

- Use academic hierarchy: abstract, research question, methodology, literature review, findings, discussion, limitations, references, and numbered table/figure captions.
- Keep evidence tables and method matrices within Word page width; split dense data by construct, sample, period, or theme.
- Use static figures with captions, source notes, and alternative descriptions; avoid interactive HTML, viewport-dependent layouts, and complex absolute positioning.
- Make long titles, citations, reference URLs, and table cells wrap cleanly.
- DOCX/PDF conversion requires separate export validation; this preset does not guarantee conversion success.
