# Academic Paper Validation Checklist

- The research question, contribution, method, and scope are explicit.
- Literature review, findings, interpretation, and limitations are separated.
- Citation style and reference expectations are visible and consistent.
- Method claims do not exceed available data or evidence.
- Direct quotes, paraphrases, data-based claims, and inferences are distinguished.
- The conclusion acknowledges limitations and does not overstate generalizability.
- Abstract, methodology, references, table/figure captions, and limitations use an academic document hierarchy.
- Abstract, methodology, results, discussion, references, data notes, and appendix blocks are used according to the paper's discipline and evidence needs.
- Methodology tables, evidence tables, and appendix material are selected only when they clarify method, data, results, or limitations.
- Long references, DOI strings, URLs, titles, and table cells are handled by semantic wrapping, splitting, shortening display text, or appendix placement without broad forced character-breaking layout rules.
- Evidence tables and method matrices remain readable within Word page width, and long citations, URLs, titles, and cells can wrap.
- Static figures include captions, source notes, and alternative descriptions.
- Interactive HTML, viewport-dependent layouts, and complex absolute positioning are avoided for exportable outputs.
- DOCX/PDF export success is not assumed until export validation is run separately.

## Design And Export-Safe Checkpoints

- AI judgment records whether the structure follows abstract/methodology/results/discussion/references or a discipline-specific variation.
- AI judgment separates source fact, coded category, statistical output, author interpretation, and inference.
- Appendix or landscape candidates are marked as export review needs, not as guaranteed DOCX/PDF behavior.
- Deferred export-native features remain deferred: Word field codes, SEQ captions, generated captions, PAGE/NUMPAGES fields, bibliography automation, journal-template automation, and landscape sections are not claimed.
