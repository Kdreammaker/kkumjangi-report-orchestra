# Press Release Validation Checklist

- The document is written as an external public release, not as a report summary.
- Headline, subheadline, lead, body, quotes, boilerplate, contact, embargo, and approval status are handled where relevant.
- Facts, figures, names, partner references, and quotes have approval status.
- Internal-only rationale, unapproved claims, and unresolved risk language are not exposed.
- Company boilerplate and contact details are present or clearly marked as needed.
- The release tone is factual, newsworthy, and publication-ready without unsupported hype.
- The document follows the short public-release flow: brief PRD, fact/approval check, draft writing, and public-risk review.
- Headline, subheadline, dateline, lead, body, quote, boilerplate, and contact have a public-document hierarchy.
- Release status is explicitly labeled as `FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE`, `EMBARGOED UNTIL...`, `DRAFT FOR REVIEW`, `즉시 배포`, or `엠바고` where relevant.
- Dateline, approved quote, boilerplate, media contact, and fact box blocks are used only when they are public-approved and useful for journalists.
- Unapproved public statements, exaggerated marketing claims, internal metrics, draft rationale, and confidential partner details are excluded from the release body.

## Prohibited Style and Judgment Patterns

- Do not expose internal risks, unresolved issues, draft-only rationale, unapproved figures, unapproved partner references, or non-cleared quotes in the public body.
- Do not convert internal confidence, roadmap intent, or negotiation status into public factual claims.
- Do not use hype to compensate for missing approval; publication-ready means approved, attributable, and externally safe.

## Design And Export-Safe Checkpoints

- AI judgment records whether the release is immediate, embargoed, review draft, or not ready for public drafting.
- AI judgment checks every fact, figure, partner reference, image, quote, and contact detail for external approval status.
- Fact boxes are kept small and public-safe; larger data or internal context is not moved into appendix as if it were release material.
- Deferred export-native features remain deferred: Word field codes, generated captions, page fields, distribution automation, embargo enforcement, approval routing, and media-kit packaging are not claimed.

## Export Readiness Checks

- Fact boxes or data tables stay within Word page width, and long names, contact details, and URLs can wrap.
- Static approved images include captions and alternative descriptions; interactive HTML, viewport-dependent layouts, and complex absolute positioning are avoided.
- DOCX/PDF export success is not assumed until export validation is run separately.
