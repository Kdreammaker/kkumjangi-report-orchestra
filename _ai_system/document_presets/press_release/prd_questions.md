# Press Release PRD Questions

- What is the news event, announcement, launch, partnership, result, or milestone?
- Who is the target media, external audience, and approving organization?
- What headline, subheadline, lead message, timing, dateline, embargo, and release status apply?
- Which facts, numbers, names, quotes, partner references, and product claims are approved for external release?
- Who provides official quotes and media/contact information?
- What company boilerplate, disclaimer, legal review, and approval status must be included?
