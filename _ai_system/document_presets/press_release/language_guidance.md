# Press Release Language Guidance

Use this file only when the selected preset is `press_release` and the PRD/current task needs language or notation guidance. Do not create `press_release_en`.

## Korean Default Guidance

- Korean press releases should separate approved facts, approved quotes, boilerplate, media contact, embargo state, and public-release approval status.
- Keep the tone factual and publishable. Do not expose unapproved internal metrics, source ids, draft caveats, or production notes.

## English Guidance

- English press releases may use conventional labels such as `FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE`, `EMBARGOED UNTIL`, dateline, approved quote blocks, `About [Company]`, and `Media Contact`.
- Use AP-style plainness where appropriate, but preserve proper nouns, titles, product names, quote wording, dates, and approved boilerplate exactly.
- `Source:` labels are rarely needed in a public release body. If supporting tables or figures are included, use English labels only when English output is explicitly marked.

## Ask Before Drafting

Ask before drafting when:

- publication timing, embargo, quote approval, boilerplate, media contact, or distribution market is unclear,
- the release mentions fundraising, securities, financial performance, regulatory approval, clinical/legal claims, partnerships, or customer names,
- the user asks to translate a Korean release into English but approved quote or boilerplate wording may differ.

## Forbidden Or Caution Expressions

- Do not invent `FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE`, embargo time, dateline, media contact, company boilerplate, or quote attribution when not provided.
- Do not make unapproved superlative claims such as `world-leading`, `first`, `only`, `revolutionary`, or `guaranteed`.
- Do not auto-generate securities, legal, regulatory, medical, or forward-looking disclaimer language.

## Genre Boundary

This preset supports an approved public announcement draft. It is not a marketing landing page, investor solicitation, legal disclosure, regulatory filing, or social media campaign by default.

## Caption, Source, Accessed, Disclaimer

- Korean output: use Korean release labels and `접근일: YYYY.MM.DD` only when reference lists are needed.
- English output: `FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE`, `EMBARGOED UNTIL`, dateline, `About`, and `Media Contact` may be used when confirmed.
- `disclaimer_profile` is guidance only. Public-release disclaimers require approval.

## Protected Spans

Preserve approved quotes, names, titles, datelines, embargo text, product names, boilerplate, contact details, financial or regulatory wording, and any approved public statement.
