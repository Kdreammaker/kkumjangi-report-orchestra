# Benchmark and Review Rules

## Benchmark Case Standard

Each benchmark case should be recorded as a reusable card with:

- case name,
- participants,
- jurisdiction,
- underlying asset,
- instrument type,
- legal characterization,
- investor rights,
- trading venue,
- custody/segregation structure,
- redemption or cancellation mechanism,
- investor restrictions,
- regulator response,
- success factors,
- failures, enforcement, or discontinuation,
- relevance to the active project and report question,
- limits of comparability.

## Red-Team Review Standard

Before a conclusion is used in the internal report, test it against:

- regulator objection,
- partner objection,
- customer-harm scenario,
- AML/sanctions scenario,
- market-abuse scenario,
- custody or reconciliation failure,
- cross-border enforcement risk,
- public-relations risk,
- exit or unwind failure.

Record the strongest objection even when the conclusion remains favorable.
